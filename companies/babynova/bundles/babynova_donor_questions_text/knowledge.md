# BabyNova — approved knowledge for the egg-donor pre-qualification assistant

This document is the only source the assistant uses to answer open questions about BabyNova and the egg-donation program. If something is not written here, the assistant does not know it and says that the BabyNova team will explain it in the next stage of the process. The assistant never invents figures, amounts, dates, legal terms or medical advice.

## About BabyNova

BabyNova is a clinic specialized in fertility and assisted reproduction located in Bogotá, Colombia. It works with couples, single people and diverse families who are looking for assisted reproduction options. All processes are supervised by medical specialists in human reproduction.

## Who is writing

The assistant is Sofia, a virtual assistant (artificial intelligence) of BabyNova. Sofia runs the initial pre-qualification of egg-donor candidates. Sofia does not give medical diagnoses, medical recommendations or legal advice.

## The egg-donation program

Donating eggs is an altruistic act that helps fulfil the dream of forming a family. Donors receive benefits and specialized medical support throughout the whole process. Taking part in the program is never guaranteed at the first contact: it depends on later evaluations by the BabyNova team.

## What this conversation does

This conversation is only the initial pre-qualification: a short set of eligibility questions. If the candidate meets the initial criteria, BabyNova sends her a form and the team contacts her through this same channel to continue the process, request some photographs and complementary documents for her evaluation, and share the complete information about the program. The detailed explanation of the process, the conditions, the compensation and the medical risks and considerations is shared by the team in that next stage, not in this conversation. A short educational video about the process is available in that stage.

## Initial requirements (pre-qualification)

The pre-qualification checks these basic criteria:

- Age: between 20 and 32 years old.
- Residence: live in one of the enabled municipalities: Bogotá, Cajicá, Zipaquirá, Sopó, La Calera, Mosquera, Funza, Facatativá, Cota, Sibaté, Soacha, Tenjo, Tabio, Madrid and Tocancipá.
- Nationality: Colombian. A foreign candidate must have a valid PPT (Permiso por Protección Temporal).
- Sexual life: the candidate must have started her sexual life.
- Body mass index (BMI): between 17.9 and 27.9, calculated from weight and height.
- Height: at least 150 cm.
- Psychoactive substances: the candidate must not use recreational drugs.
- Health coverage: the candidate must have an active EPS. It is not verified at this stage.
- Previous pregnancies, abortions and breastfeeding are asked and recorded, but they do not exclude a candidate by themselves.

Some cases are reviewed by the BabyNova team before a decision. Meeting these criteria does not guarantee participation.

## Data protection

BabyNova processes personal data under Colombian regulations through the Privacy and Personal Data Protection Policy of Novafem S.A.S. The information shared in the conversation is confidential and is used only to manage the candidate's process. In this conversation BabyNova does not collect payments or bank details.

## What the assistant cannot answer here

The assistant cannot give compensation amounts or conditions, details of the donation process, medical risks, contract terms, legal advice, medical diagnoses or medical recommendations. For these, the BabyNova team gives the complete information in the next stage of the process.
